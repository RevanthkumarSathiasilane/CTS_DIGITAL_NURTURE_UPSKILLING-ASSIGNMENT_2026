Help and FAQs

Find answers to common questions about using the portal.

How to Use This Portal

  1. Open the Home page and browse upcoming events
  2. Click on any event to see full details
  3. Click Register Now to go to the registration form
  4. Fill in your details and click Register
  5. Use the Services page for emergency contacts and city services
  6. Use the Contact page to reach the city council directly

Frequently Asked Questions

Q: Is registration free?

Yes, registration for all events through this portal is completely free of charge.

Q: Can I register for multiple events?

Yes, you can register for as many events as you like by filling the registration form separately for each event.

Q: How will I know my registration is confirmed?

After submitting the form you will see a confirmation message on the page. The city council will also contact you via the email you provided.

Q: What if I face issues with the portal?

Please use the Contact page to send a message to the city council team and they will respond within 24 hours.
